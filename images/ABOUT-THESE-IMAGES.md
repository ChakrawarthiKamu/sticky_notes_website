# About these images

All 97 JPGs in this folder are **3D renders created for this build** (modelled and rendered with Blender). They are not photographs of real products or real homes. Product images match each product page (colours, shapes, sizes, printed layouts); the `lifestyle/` scenes show notes in use on a fridge, a front door, a mirror, around a TV, on a storyboard wall and in a book.

To use your own photography, save a JPG over the same file name. Each product has three images: `-01` (main shot, also the card image), `-02` (flat lay, shown on card hover) and `-03` (close-up).
