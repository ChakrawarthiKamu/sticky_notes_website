About Notera

Built for sticky note lovers.

We make one thing. From the everyday yellow square to hard-to-find clear film and week-view pads, Notera exists to make good sticky notes easier to discover and buy.

Every pad is 80 gsm FSC-certified paper with a water-based adhesive that holds on glass, paint and screens, and lifts off without a mark.

How it works

Notera. Stick. Repeat.

Notera

Lift a sheet from the side, not the bottom, and it stays flat instead of curling.

Stick

Press along the adhesive strip. It holds on paper, glass, paint and laptop lids.

Repeat

Move it as plans change. Each sheet re-sticks many times and leaves nothing behind.
